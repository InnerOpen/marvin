# Alt text examples

| Image | Weak | Better | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| A close-up of a hand-thrown bowl with a crack in the glaze | `image of a bowl` | `Hand-thrown bowl with a hairline crack running through the blue glaze.` | Names the subject and the detail that matters. |
| A photo of a dog on a beach, in a post about the beach | `IMG_2041.jpg` | `A brown dog running through shallow surf at low tide.` | Says what is happening, not the file name. |
| A screenshot of a settings page, in a how-to | `screenshot` | `The Storage settings page with the Check connection button highlighted.` | Describes what the reader is meant to notice. |
| A bar chart of monthly visitors | `chart of visitors` | `Bar chart: monthly visitors rose from 1,200 in March to 2,900 in June.` | Gives the point the chart makes. |
| A banner with the words "Open studio, Saturday 10 to 4" | `banner` | `Banner: "Open studio, Saturday 10 to 4".` | Transcribes the text, which is the information. |
| A thin line between two sections | `divider` | _(empty)_ | Decorative: nothing to say, so a screen reader skips it. |
| A logo next to the company name in the header | `logo` | _(empty)_ or `Company name` if the name isn't written beside it | The name beside it already says it. |
| A product photo whose caption lists size and colour | `Photo of a red mug, 350 ml, ceramic` | `Red ceramic mug on a wooden table.` | The caption has the specs; the alt text doesn't repeat them. |

## Too long

> A photograph taken in the afternoon in the studio showing a pair of hands holding a lump of wet clay on a spinning
> wheel while the potter leans over it and water drips from the edge of the clay onto the splash pan below.

Better: `Hands shaping wet clay on a potter's wheel.`
